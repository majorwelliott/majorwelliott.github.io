Manager View

Managers are able to browse, edit, and create stock, accounts, and discounts. They can view auto-created reports on sales and inventory. Logging in as any account through point-of-sales, managers can also place purchases for customers.

Account Creation

Customers may create an account, which allows them to view their previous purchases and receive receipts through through their given email.

Purchase Finalization

After adding items to their cart, customers will be able to edit their cart, enter a discount code, and select their preferred shipping method. They will then finalize their order by entering their credit card information and address.
