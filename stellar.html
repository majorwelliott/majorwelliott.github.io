Gameplay

• Move with the mouse
• Get score by destroying enemies
• You have 3 lives
• Power-ups will spawn randomly, your lasers will destroy them, so fly into them
• Avoid enemy ships and the skull power-ups

Power-ups:
• Shield - protects you from damage
• Bomb - destroys all enemies on the screen
• Laser - upgrades your laser output
• Skull - damages you and removes a life
